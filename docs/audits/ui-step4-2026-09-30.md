# Step 4 screens review, 2026-09-30 (T29)

A reviewer pass over step 4's screens only (plan `2026-09-30-step-4-household.md`, Task 29), not
the second UI audit. Checked against the design board (`docs/design/kept-screens.html`) and the
screens spec §3, §5 and §8, with the step-4 carry-over list folded in. Every high and medium
finding is fixed; the lows are listed at the end, nine fixed afterwards and one kept.

**Result: 0 high, 9 medium (all fixed), 10 low (9 fixed, 1 kept).**

## How it was run

- **A real server, not the mock.** A worktree of HEAD, built with
  `pnpm --filter '@kept/server...' build && pnpm --filter @kept/web build`, served by
  `node --conditions=kept-dist apps/web/e2e/serve.mjs reviewfour 8397 --seed`: a scratch database
  on the dev Postgres (5452), the `households` seed, dropped when the server stopped.
- **Step 4's data through the API**, since the seed has none yet (T30 extends it): three warranties
  (one ending in 12 days, one lifetime, one with a registration deadline in 5 days), a claim in
  repair, two valuations (EGP and USD), loans (the drill to Murdock three days overdue, 2 of 3 HDMI
  cables to Louis, Catan with no due date, an Arabic one in بيت العائلة, a borrowed ladder), six
  schedules (overdue, due, one-off, on a place, a car's 10,000 km, an Arabic one), four expiring
  documents (one expired, one of the location itself, one in Arabic), a burglary with two stolen
  things, two exchange rates. Then one pass of the reminder scan (`dist/reminders/scan.js
  runScan`, as `e2e/step4.spec.ts` does): 12 occurrences, 24 notifications.
- **Headless scripted Playwright** (Chromium, `--use-fake-ui-for-media-stream
  --use-fake-device-for-media-stream`), never the MCP browser tools. 29 routes: Home, Schedules,
  Lending (out and in), Paperwork, Expiring, Notifications, Me → Notifications, Incidents, an
  incident, the insurance report, the claim pack, exchange rates, a thing on each step-4 tab (the
  TV: warranties, value, claims; the drill: loans; the lent part; the borrowed ladder; a schedule;
  the car's meter schedule), a contact's page, a location and a place; plus the Arabic location's
  pages. Variants: Ibrahim at 375 and 1280, light and dark, English and Arabic; Talia (viewer);
  Alfred (Arabic).
- **Probes on every page:** horizontal scroll, text cut with an ellipsis or a line clamp, native
  `<select>`, touch targets under 44 px at 375 (with the kit's `::after` hit area), Latin text left
  in Arabic outside `<bdi>`, console errors, and axe (`@axe-core/playwright`, WCAG 2.0–2.2 A/AA and
  best practices, nothing below moderate reported).
- **By hand:** JPEG screenshots of the screens above, the module-off state (Garage with Lending,
  Schedules, Warranties and Money switched off), and offline (with the service worker: a household
  chunk never loaded; writes on a loaded thing page).

## What passed as it was

- **axe:** no violation at moderate or above on any step-4 route, in any variant.
- **Phones:** no horizontal scroll at 375; nothing cut with …; no native `<select>`; every
  control at least 44 × 44.
- **Viewer (Talia):** no Mark returned, Complete, More, Edit or Update on her lists or her thing
  page; money and Garage's rows stay out as the server sends them.
- **Offline:** on a loaded thing page, Mark returned and the other writes are disabled with
  "Needs a connection"; an on-demand screen never loaded before says "Needs a connection" in its
  frame.
- **Module off:** a thing's Loans, Value, Claims and Schedules tabs go with their modules (T20's
  rule), Warranties says "Off in this location" with Turn on, Lend in the action menu says "Off in
  this location", and the derived "with Murdock since…" chip stays in the header.
- **RTL:** step-4 lists, sheets and the notification centre mirror; a Latin name inside an Arabic
  title (`Samsung TV, 55″ · الضمان`) keeps its order.

breaks the spec or an accessibility or language rule on some routes or for some people; **low** is
