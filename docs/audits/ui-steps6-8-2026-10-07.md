# Steps 6–8 screens review, 2026-10-07

One combined reviewer pass over the screens of steps 6, 7 and 8, in place of step 6 T25's UI part,
step 7's review, and step 8's T24 operations review and T26 second UI audit for these screens. Run
as step 5's was (`ui-step5-2026-10-06.md`), with an operations pass beside it (plain-HTTP refusals
and the start against a slow Postgres). Every high and medium finding is fixed; the lows are listed
at the end.

**Result: 2 high, 9 medium (all fixed), 2 operations findings (fixed), 18 low (kept).**

## How it was run

- **A real server, not the mock.** A worktree of HEAD, built with
  `pnpm --filter '@kept/server...' build && pnpm --filter @kept/web build`, served by
  `apps/web/e2e/serve.mjs` (a copy that takes the public URL from the environment) on 8473 with
  `--seed --ai-mock`: a scratch database on the dev Postgres (5452), the `households` seed, dropped
  when the server stopped. **The seed now completes** (step 6's 500 on "Replace the water filter"
  is gone; nothing to fix). Most of the review ran behind a local TLS proxy with an `https:` public
  URL, so the recovery kit, exports and tokens could run; the plain-HTTP checks ran on a second
  start with an `http:` public URL. Rebuilt and re-run after the fixes.
- **Set up for the review**, in the scratch database: the mock AI provider (the e2e's made-up key)
  on Ibrahim's and Alfred's accounts; Consumables on in Home; stock rules ("keep at least 50") on
  the AA batteries and the water filter, so both are low; for the webhook deliveries,
  `ssrf_allow_private` on and a hook to a closed local port. Two scripted mock cases added to the
  worktree's `test/fixtures/assistant/cases.json` (not committed) for D213's spoken list, English
  (Ibrahim, Home) and Arabic (Alfred, بيت العائلة): `add_thing` with three items and a new place.
- **Headless scripted Playwright** (Chromium, `--use-fake-ui-for-media-stream
  --use-fake-device-for-media-stream`), never the MCP browser tools. **Dictation used a stub
  recogniser only** (`window.SpeechRecognition` and `webkitSpeechRecognition` replaced before the
  app loaded; no real recogniser was ever started), and the app lock a stubbed WebAuthn (PRF).
- **Screens:** the assistant sheet (375) and panel (768, 1024, 1280, 1440), find answers, dictation,
  the D213 card (tick, untick, Confirm, Undo), a move card made stale by a rename (conflict), an
  injection case, threads, offline; Talia's write question; Settings → Connections (make a token,
  D179's two-location warning, the token once, Done, Revoke, a change made through the public API
  with a token in Recent changes); the OAuth consent page (its view intercepted: there is no public
  CIMD client here); location webhooks (add, the secret once, test, deliveries); Search's
  semantic notes; AI settings and Admin → AI; Import (CSV, the Homebox ZIP from
  `apps/server/test/fixtures/homebox/`, and a Kept export made in the review, with secrets and a
  passphrase); Labels; Export (with secrets and passphrase, ready, download); Consumables, low stock,
  Home and the thing page; field conversion; Admin → Backups; the status page; the update line; the
  recovery kit download with re-authentication; This device (PIN, keep offline, the lock screen
  after a reload); full width at 1920 on every settings tab.
- **Variants:** Ibrahim at 375 and 1280, dark at 375 and 1280, Arabic (Ibrahim with Arabic) at 375
  and 1280, 1920 full width, Alfred (Arabic) at 375 and 1280, Talia (viewer) at 375 and 1280, Louis
  on consent.
- **Probes on every page and sheet:** horizontal scroll, text cut with an ellipsis or a line clamp,
  native `<select>`, touch targets under 44 px at 375 (with the kit's `::after` hit area), Latin text
  and Western digits left in Arabic outside an isolate (and in inputs), console errors, and axe
  (`@axe-core/playwright`, WCAG 2.0–2.2 A/AA and best practices, moderate and above).

## What passed as it was

- No horizontal scroll, nothing cut with …, no native `<select>` on any probed page at 375, 1280 or
  1920; no console errors other than the expected 4xx of refused requests; axe clean on every
  static page in every variant before the fixes, and on the cards after them.
- **Dictation:** the stub's interim text shows in the field, the final list stays, `lang` is the bare
  interface language (`en`, `ar`); the spoken list becomes one card with three rows and one new place,
  in English and in Arabic (Eastern digits, ٢ ×), and one Confirm adds them with one Undo.
- **Viewer:** Talia asking from Home gets "Viewers can't make changes here. Ask an admin of Home.",
  no card; her token sheet offers read only.
- **Tokens:** the secret shows once and is gone from the DOM after Done; Revoke asks in Kept's own
  dialog.
- **Exports and imports:** an export with secrets is made, ready and downloads; the Kept export
  imports back into a new location with its passphrase; the CSV import maps, checks and imports.
- **Recovery kit:** downloads after re-entering the password; the status page then says it's kept.
- **This device:** the PIN keypad, keep offline with its warning, and the lock screen after a reload.
- **Semantic search:** with the mock (no embeddings model) Search says "Keyword search only here (no
  embeddings model)" in both languages; "matched by meaning" itself isn't reachable without a real
  embeddings model (L10).
