## How it was run

- **A real server, not the mock.** A worktree of HEAD, built with
  `pnpm --filter '@kept/server...' build && pnpm --filter @kept/web build`, served by
  `node --conditions=kept-dist apps/web/e2e/serve.mjs reviewfive 8471 --seed --ai-mock`: a scratch
  database on the dev Postgres (5452), the `households` seed (the Corolla, the Hyundai, the
  generator and step 4's rows), dropped when the server stopped. Rebuilt and re-run once after the
  fixes: after them no `km` and no English History field is left in Arabic, no Western digit in a
  prefilled field, and axe reports only L1 on the step-5 sheets. One rerun of the dark phone sheets
  timed out on the loaded box (L8); the first pass had them clean.
- **Set up for the review**, in the scratch database: Talia added to Garage as a viewer (the seed
  has no viewer there); Louis's Home membership removed, so he has no vehicle and sees Add a
  vehicle; the generator's readings moved 12 days back with a 7-day nudge, so a "Reading needed"
  row reaches Home and Expiring; the mock AI provider set on Ibrahim's and Alfred's accounts (the
  e2e's made-up key); two fixture images keyed in the worktree's mock answers, an invoice (three
  lines: fluid, part, labour) and a registration card (registration, expiring in 20 days), the
  card read in LABEL mode on the Corolla through `POST /things/:id/extract`.
- **Headless scripted Playwright** (Chromium, `--use-fake-ui-for-media-stream
  --use-fake-device-for-media-stream`), never the MCP browser tools. Routes: `/vehicles`, Home,
  `/expiring?f.source=reading`, `/inbox`, the Corolla's page (one page on a phone; each of the
  seven tabs from 768 up), and Alfred's Hyundai and generator. Sheets: Log a reading (empty, lower,
  a jump over the daily limit, fits), Log a service (empty, with the invoice read, after Confirm
  all), Log fuel, Add a document, the history report, Add a vehicle. Variants: Ibrahim at 375, 768
  and 1280; dark at 375 and 1280; Arabic at 375 and 1280; Alfred (Arabic) at 375 and 1280; Talia
  (viewer) at 375 and 1280; Louis for Add a vehicle (English 375, Arabic 1280).
- **Probes on every page and sheet:** horizontal scroll, text cut with an ellipsis or a line clamp,
  native `<select>`, touch targets under 44 px at 375 (with the kit's `::after` hit area), Latin
  text and Western digits left in Arabic outside `<bdi>` (and in inputs), console errors, and axe
  (`@axe-core/playwright`, WCAG 2.0–2.2 A/AA and best practices, moderate and above reported).
- **States:** offline (the vehicle page loaded, then offline: Log a reading saves on the phone in
  English and Arabic; `/vehicles` never loaded says "Needs a connection"); module off (Vehicles off
  in Garage: the Corolla leaves `/vehicles` and shows as a plain metered thing); a viewer (Talia: no
  Log, Add, Edit or Read the card; no amounts on Costs); a keyboard walk at 1280 (Vehicles → Corolla by Tab and Enter; Log a reading, Log fuel and Log a
  service each reached by Tab, opened by Enter with focus inside the sheet, Escape back to the
  trigger; History report reached by Tab and opened by Enter).
