# The final check's performance figures (steps 5–8)

Measured on 2026-10-07 in the combined final check for steps 5–8, on the full gate's `perf` step
(`scripts/ci-local.sh perf`: the snapshot bench, then `apps/server/test/perf` through
`vitest.perf.config.ts`, one file at a time). It ran 10:23–10:32 Cairo with no other test run on
the machine or its Postgres (the only agent; load average 5–12, most of it the host's own
processes). `test/perf/steps6-8.perf.test.ts` was also run on its own before the gate (09:50–09:56,
load 6–8); its figures there were within 10% of the gate's below.

Every limit passes. p50 / p95 / max in ms; 5 warm-ups, then 50 timed requests (the scan: 3 passes),
through the app in-process (fastify inject: server time, no network).

## The fixture

Step 4's household (`test/perf/household.ts`): Home with 10,000 things in 20 places, 2,000
warranties, 500 schedules, 300 loans, 200 documents. `vehicles.perf.test.ts` adds 49 cars with
starter schedules and readings, and the Corolla with five years of fills and services (see
[2026-10-06-step5.md](2026-10-06-step5.md)). `steps6-8.perf.test.ts` adds an embeddings model on
Home's account (the mock provider's concept vectors, never a real one), all 10,000 things embedded
by the backfill as the worker does it, MCP on in Home, and Louis's read token.

Recorded without a limit, the worst case for ranking: a query with a word all 10,000 names share
("Thing 04217", every row matches and is ranked): keyword search 459 / 476.8 / 527, meaning fused in
("the thing for the TV") 356.2 / 368.7 / 377.5, MCP `where_is` 462.7 / 485.2 / 503.5. These are over
§3.1's 300 ms search budget; the fixture's names are artificial (every one "Thing NNNNN"), and a real
inventory's common words match far fewer rows. Noted, not carried as a failure.

The backfill embedded the 10,000 things in 4 hourly runs (40 batches a location a run,
`BATCHES_PER_LOCATION`), 278 s in all on the mock embedder.

## Notes

- **"Export of the seed" is the perf household, not the `households` seed.** The `households` seed
  is 35 things in Home; the 10,000-thing household is the figure that says something. It has no
  files, so the plan's "export of 10,000 things with 2 GB of files" and the 2 GB VM's RSS budget
  (`--max-old-space-size=512`) are not measured here (step-7 carry-over).
- **The meaning search's query found no thing by meaning** on these rows (the mock's concept
  vectors know cables and TVs; the fixture's things are all "Thing NNNNN"): the figure is the cost
  of embedding the query and the vector scan over 10,000 vectors fused with keywords, not recall.
  Recall is the search evaluation's (`pnpm eval:search`, in the gate's `eval` step).
- **"Scan" is the reminder scan pass.** A label scan's resolve (`POST /api/v1/scan/resolve`) has no
  perf check; it is one indexed lookup.
- The Homebox import is the committed fixture's 8 things; the plan's 10,000-entity import is not
  measured (no such fixture).
