Screens · Search, assistant & notifications

Search, assistant and notifications

Finding things, asking about them, and hearing when something needs you. Search is plain Postgres with visible aliases and never waits on a model (D41, D42). The assistant is a calm sheet that knows the page; it changes nothing until you confirm a card that Kept draws from the tool's arguments, which model text cannot imitate (D22–D24, D179). Every change lands in the activity feed with undo, secrets appear there without their values, and a clash between two people is settled field by field (D110, D150, D156).
