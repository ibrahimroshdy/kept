More, Help, and a tour that walks every section

The phone's More page, where everything off the tab bar lives. Help as built: the tour, the checklist brought back, installing, sharing in, an odometer Shortcut and Diagnostics. "Show me around" steps through each section the person has (Home, Capture, the Inbox, a location, Search, then every module that is on), highlighting one part of each. It describes; it never asks for a tap that leaves its path, because any page it didn't open ends it. At the end, anything it added can be kept or discarded.
