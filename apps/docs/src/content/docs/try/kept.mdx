## What to try

- **Home**: what needs you. Tap a count to open that list, already filtered.
- A location: its rooms and what's in them, down to the box. **Map** shows it in 3D.
- A thing: its paperwork, warranty, history and label.
- **Search**: part of a name, such as `cable`.
- **Labels**: the things with no printed label, on a sheet.
- **Vehicles**, **Lending**, **Expiring** and **Insights** in the sidebar, or **More** on a phone.

The camera, the microphone, notifications and the app lock are off here. **Reset** in the line
at the top brings the sample back. To run Kept on your own server, start with
[Install with Docker Compose](/install/compose/). Only the map: [Try the map](/try/map/).
