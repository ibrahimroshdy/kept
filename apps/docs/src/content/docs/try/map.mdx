## What to try

- Tap a room, a shelf or a box: the card says where it is and what's inside. **Details** has the rest.
- **Plan** looks straight down. The floor buttons (**G**, **F**, **R**) go up and down; **All** stacks them.
- **Low stock**, **Lent out** and **Due soon** turn their pins on and off.
- **Arrange**: drag things, resize a room by its amber corner, draw a room. **Done** keeps it until you leave the page.
- Turn off **Detailed models** and the map downloads no models at all.

This is the map Kept ships, with the same models. [The location map](/users/location-map/) explains
how a real location gets its floors and rooms. The whole app with a sample household:
